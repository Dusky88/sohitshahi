About Sohit

Hi, I'm a certified personal coach with a lifelong passion for fitness, health, and helping others reach their full potential. Coming from a military background, I learned the values of discipline, dedication, and perseverance early in life.

These principles have shaped both my personal fitness journey and my approach to coaching clients. I believe that fitness is more than just exercise — it's about building confidence, developing mental strength, and creating healthy habits that last a lifetime.

Whether your goal is to lose weight, build muscle, improve endurance, or enhance overall well-being, I am committed to providing personalized guidance, motivation, and support to help you achieve lasting results.
